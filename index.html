3 FLOORS OF PURE POWER

Every membership unlocks unlimited access to all three specialized floors, engineered for cardio endurance, strength training, and athletic performance.

Floor 1

Cardio Floor

Treadmills, curved runners, rowing machines, and air bikes to build conditioning and burn fat efficiently.

Floor 2

Strength Training

Olympic barbells, power racks, platforms, and calibrated plates built for serious lifters chasing PRs.

Floor 3

Functional Training Area

Turf tracks, sleds, battle ropes, plyo boxes, and kettlebells — train movements that translate into raw power.

Free Weights

Free Weights Area

Extensive dumbbell rack (2.5kg to 50kg+), adjustable benches, and EZ curl bars for precision hypertrophy.

Coaching

Personal Coaching

Dedicated 1-on-1 coaching spaces with form-check mirrors, movement screens, and tailored workout sessions.

Recovery

Mobility and Recovery

Foam rollers, resistance bands, yoga mats, and stretching cages for active recovery and injury prevention.
