About ORIGIN

Where Every
Rep Matters.

ORIGIN isn't just a gym — it's a dedicated 3-floor training destination located at Trios Tower, Ambattur, Chennai. Built for lifters, athletes, and anyone committed to achieving true strength and conditioning.

Our philosophy is simple: provide a no-compromise environment across 3 specialised levels featuring dedicated zones for Cardio, Strength & Free Weights, and Functional Movement. No gimmicks. No shortcuts. Just results.

From competition-grade barbells and squat racks to turf sleds and high-end cardio machines, every detail at ORIGIN is engineered for real, measurable progress.

Our Space

3 Floors of World-Class Facilities.

Spread across 3 dedicated training levels at Trios Tower, Ambattur — purpose-built zones for Strength, Cardio, and Functional conditioning.

Strength Training

Competition-grade racks, platforms, and free weights. Built for serious lifters.

Cardio Zone

State-of-the-art treadmills, rowers, and bikes for endurance work.

Functional Training

Open floor space with turf, sleds, ropes, and movement tools.

Free Weights

A dedicated zone with a full dumbbell range, Olympic bars, and plate-loaded machines.

PT Studio

Private training space for focused one-on-one coaching sessions.
